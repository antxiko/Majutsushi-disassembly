A Japanese four-player mahjong with eight Konami characters as rivals, whose playing styles adapt to the hands you win: the yaku measured by running its own code, the rules, the digital voices taken from the ROM and the whole cartridge explained.

What is inside

The game

  • The game: the screens in order, from the title to the payment.
  • The rivals: the eight characters and the seven counters that give each its style.
  • The rules: the ten options and their defaults.
  • The yaku: every hand the game recognises, measured by running it.
  • The voices: the eight digital voices, decoded from the ROM.
  • The music: the fourteen PSG tunes and effects, played by the cartridge's own engine.

Curiosities

  • Curiosities: the false-win penalty, the free space, a cartridge with a DAC.

Under the hood

  • Under the hood: rebuilding the ROM, the mapper, the state machine, the hands in RAM, the data banks.
